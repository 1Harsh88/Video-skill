# Fliki "AI Animation Generator": what it offers (reference)

Source: https://fliki.ai/features/ai-animation-generator, read on 2026-11-17. These are Fliki's own claims and may have changed since.

## What Fliki does
- **Input:** an idea, a script, a blog URL, a PPT/PDF, product marketing, translate, or edit video. You also pick language, tone and duration (up to 15 min).
- **Script:** written scene by scene, with animated visuals made "per line".
- **Style presets:** Anime, Comic Book, Fantasy, 2D Cartoon, Live-action, Cinematic. You can also upload reference images to lock a custom style.
- **Voice:** 2,000+ AI voices in 80+ languages; voice cloning on paid plans.
- **Extras:**
  - AI avatar host with lip-sync (paid)
  - auto captions in 80+ languages
  - royalty-free music that ducks under the voice, plus an SFX library
- **Editing:** timeline editor, brand kit (paid), and one-click translation into other languages (paid).
- **Export:** MP4 in 9:16, 1:1 or 16:9. 720p with a watermark on the free plan; 1080p and 4K on paid plans.

## Limits and pricing (as stated)
- **Free plan:** 3 credits/month (1 credit = 1 min of video), 720p, watermark, no commercial use.
- **Paid plans:** from $8/month (FAQ); the watermark is removed from $28/month (video generator page).
- **Speed:** a 60 s animation takes about 3–6 min to generate.

## How this skill maps to Fliki

| Fliki feature | Here |
